# Privacy Policy

**App:** Bitcoin Ticker
**Effective date:** August 12, 2026

Bitcoin Ticker ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information.**

The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not collect, store, sell, or share your name, email, location, device identifiers, contacts, or any other personal data. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Network Requests to Exchanges

To display the current Bitcoin price, the app requests publicly available price data directly from third-party cryptocurrency exchange APIs, such as:

- Binance
- Bitfinex
- Bitstamp
- Coinbase
- CoinGecko
- CryptoCompare
- Gemini
- Kraken

These requests retrieve only public market price data. They do not include any personal information about you. As with any internet request, the exchange you connect to may receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address; this is handled by that exchange under its own privacy policy, not by us. We do not receive or store this information.

## Data Storage

The only data the app stores is your chosen market data source (which exchange to use), saved locally on your device. This setting never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
