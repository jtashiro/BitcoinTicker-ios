```
Bitcoin Ticker turns your iPhone or iPad into a dedicated, always-on Bitcoin price display.

A clean full-screen readout shows the current BTC/USD price in large, glanceable type, alongside the time and date — perfect for your desk, nightstand, or shelf.

LIVE PRICE
• BTC/USD price refreshes automatically every 60 seconds
• Big, bold, easy-to-read display designed to be seen from across the room
• Built-in clock and date

CHOOSE YOUR SOURCE
Pick the exchange you trust from eight supported market data sources:
• Binance
• Bitfinex
• Bitstamp
• Coinbase
• CoinGecko
• CryptoCompare
• Gemini
• Kraken

If your chosen exchange is temporarily unavailable, the app automatically falls back to another source so the price keeps updating.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account required
• No sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• No personal data collected

Bitcoin Ticker does one thing and does it well: shows you the price of Bitcoin, clearly and continuously.

Disclaimer: Prices are provided by third-party exchanges for informational purposes only and may be delayed. Bitcoin Ticker is not a trading app and does not provide financial advice.
```
